A fitness photoshoot records months, sometimes years, of work. A little planning makes sure the photos show it. This is how we help clients prepare for a [gym and fitness photoshoot in Bangalore](/Fitness-Photography), whether you are a coach building your brand, a competitor after a show, or someone proud of a transformation.

## 1. Pick the date around your peak

If you have just finished a competition or a cut, **book the shoot soon after**, before your body returns to its usual condition. For a brand or website shoot, choose a week when training and sleep are consistent rather than a week of travel or deadlines.

Any diet, water or carbohydrate changes before a shoot should be planned with your own coach or nutritionist. We photograph the result; we don't prescribe the protocol.

## 2. Decide what the photos are for

Tell us where the images will be used, because it changes the lighting, the framing and the outfits:

- **Instagram and content:** a mix of vertical frames, motion shots and close-ups.
- **Coach or trainer branding:** clean portraits for your website, plus action shots coaching or training.
- **Transformation or milestone:** consistent, well-lit physique shots that show definition.
- **Competition and bodybuilding:** high-contrast studio lighting and classic poses.

## 3. Studio or your gym?

We shoot at our studio or on-site at your gym. The **studio** gives full lighting control and high-contrast, editorial results. **Your gym** adds context, equipment and the space your clients recognise. Many coaches choose the gym for branding and the studio for physique shots.

## 4. Outfits and kit

- Bring **4–5 outfits** for your outfit changes: fitted training wear, a branded set if you have one, and one darker, solid outfit.
- Solid colours photograph better than heavy patterns. Check for pilling and loose threads.
- Bring your own props if they're part of your story: gloves, a belt, bands, a yoga mat.
- Also bring {{fitness.bringKit}} (our studio checklist), plus a comfortable layer to wear between sets.

## 5. Grooming and skin

Trim or style hair a few days before. Moisturise in the days before the shoot, and bring body oil and a water spray on the day: a controlled sheen helps show definition under studio lights.

## 6. Warm up on set

A short pump with bands or light weights just before shooting brings out muscle definition. We plan the session so the most important shots happen while you're warm. Then you show up, we guide every pose, and we light every frame.

Edited photos are delivered within 4–5 days. Plan your posts or website update for after that.
