"How much is a headshot?" is the first question most people ask, and the honest answer is: it depends on what you need the photo to do. This guide explains what changes the price of a professional headshot in Bangalore, and exactly what is included in ours, so you can compare like with like.

## What changes the price of a headshot

When you compare photographers, the headline price rarely tells the whole story. These are the things that actually move it:

- **Retouching.** Some sessions include only basic colour correction; others include advanced retouching of skin, stray hair and clothing. Ask how many photos are retouched, and to what level.
- **How many photos you get.** Some photographers charge per final image. Others give you everything from the session.
- **Backgrounds and looks.** One outfit on one background is the simplest option. Extra outfits, backgrounds or locations take more time.
- **Studio or on-site.** A studio session is usually the most efficient. On-location shoots at your office or home include travel and setup time.
- **Turnaround time.** If you need photos for a launch or a profile update this week, check delivery times before you book.
- **One person or a team.** Team headshots are usually quoted per team rather than per person, because the setup is shared.

## Is a professional headshot worth it?

A headshot is often the first impression a recruiter, client or investor has of you, and you will use it for years: on LinkedIn, your company website, speaker profiles, proposals and email signatures. Spread over that time, a well-made headshot costs very little per day, and it works for you every time someone looks you up.

The cheapest option is not always the best value. Look at a photographer's portfolio, check how much retouching is included, and make sure you will get files sized for the places you will use them.
