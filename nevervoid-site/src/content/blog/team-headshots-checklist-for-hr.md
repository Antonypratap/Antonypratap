A team headshot day can be smooth and quick, or a scramble of missed slots and mismatched photos. The difference is almost always in the planning. If you are the person in HR, people operations or the office who has been asked to organise headshots, this checklist is for you.

## Two to three weeks before

- **Decide where the photos will be used.** Website team page, LinkedIn, ID cards, pitch decks? This decides the crop, background and style.
- **Choose the look.** A plain grey or white background is timeless and easy to match later. A softly blurred office background feels more personal. Pick one and use it for everyone.
- **Agree a dress code.** Business formal, smart casual or brand colours. Consistency matters more than formality.
- **Get a headcount.** Include remote staff who could come in on the day, and leaders who may need extra time.
- **Book a date and get a quote.** Team shoots are quoted on team size, looks and location. See [corporate photography](/Corporate-photoshoot) for how it works.

## The space you need

For an on-site shoot, we bring a portable studio setup. You will need:

- **A meeting room or quiet open area** with room for a backdrop and lights, away from foot traffic
- **Power sockets** nearby
- **A chair and a mirror** for people to check their hair and clothing
- **A small waiting area** outside, so the next person is ready

## Scheduling

- **Book people in short, fixed slots**, with a calendar invite each. Each person only needs a few minutes in front of the camera.
- **Group by team or floor** so people are not walking across the office.
- **Leave buffer slots** for latecomers and anyone who wants a retake.
- **Schedule leadership separately**, often at the start of the day, as they may want more time or a second look.

## Tell the team in advance

Send a short note a week before, and a reminder the day before:

- Their slot time and location
- The dress code (with an example photo if possible)
- Tips: solid colours, no busy patterns, pressed clothes, glasses are fine
- A link to [what to wear for a professional headshot](/blog/what-to-wear-for-a-professional-headshot)

## On the day

- **Have one point of contact** who knows the schedule and can find people.
- **Keep a list** of who has been photographed, to send to the photographer for file naming.
- **Encourage people to relax.** We guide every pose and expression, so nobody needs experience.

## After the shoot

- **Selections:** each person picks their favourites, and we retouch them to a consistent finish.
- **File naming:** ask for files named by employee name, to save hours of sorting.
