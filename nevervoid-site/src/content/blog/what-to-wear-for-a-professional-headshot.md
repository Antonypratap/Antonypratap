Your headshot is usually the first thing people see of you online, on LinkedIn, your company website or a speaker profile. Clothing is the part of the photo you control completely, so it is worth getting right. This is the advice we give everyone before a [headshot session in Bangalore](/Headshot-photographer-bangalore).

## Start with where the photo will be used

Dress for the audience who will see the headshot, not for the shoot itself.

- **Corporate, finance, law, consulting:** a well-fitted blazer or suit with a plain shirt.
- **Tech, startups, product and design roles:** smart casual. A crisp shirt, a fine-knit sweater, or a blazer over a plain tee.
- **Coaches, creators and personal brands:** what you would wear to meet a client, with one signature element (a colour, a jacket) people can recognise.

If you are unsure, bring two sets: one business and one casual. That is what we recommend on our [headshot page](/Headshot-photographer-bangalore), and it gives you options for different platforms.

## Colours that work

- **Solid, mid-tone colours** such as navy, charcoal, deep green, burgundy and soft blue flatter almost every skin tone.
- **Avoid busy patterns**, thin stripes and small checks. They can shimmer on screens.
- **Avoid logos and slogans.** They date the photo and pull attention away from your face.
- **White and black** both work, but pair them with a layer so the photo doesn't look flat.
- Think about the background. We shoot on grey and white backgrounds, so a darker or richer colour will help you stand out.

## Necklines and fit

A headshot is mostly head and shoulders, so the neckline does most of the work. Collars, V-necks and structured jackets give a clean shape. Make sure clothes are **pressed and fit at the shoulders**. Creases and loose shoulders show more in a close crop than in a mirror.

## Grooming

- Get a haircut or beard trim **a few days before**, not the same morning, so it settles.
- Bring a comb, lip balm and blotting paper. The studio lights are warm.
- Glasses are fine if you normally wear them. Tell us, and we'll angle the lights to reduce reflections.
- Sleep well the night before. Rest does more for a headshot than any edit.
