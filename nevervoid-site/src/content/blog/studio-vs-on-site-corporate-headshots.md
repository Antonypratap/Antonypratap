When a company refreshes its website, launches a team page or onboards a leadership group, headshots usually come up. The first decision is where to shoot. Both options work well. They solve different problems.

## Studio headshots

Individuals come to the studio, which has controlled lighting and a seamless background.

**Best for:** founders, leadership teams, a handful of people, or anyone who wants the most polished result.

- Full control over light, so every photo matches.
- A quiet space away from the office, which helps people relax.
- Easy to reshoot new joiners later in exactly the same style.

See examples on our [studio corporate portrait](/Studio-corporate-portrait-photography) page.

## On-site headshots

We set up a portable studio in a meeting room or open area at your office.

**Best for:** larger teams, busy schedules, and companies that want an environmental look.

- No travel for staff. People step out for a few minutes and go back to work.
- Background options: a portable grey or white backdrop, or a softly blurred office.
- Can be combined with team photos or [corporate event coverage](/Corporate-event-photoshoot).

## Keeping a team consistent

Whichever option you choose, consistency is what makes a team page look professional:

1. Agree a **dress code** in advance (business, smart casual, or brand colours).
2. Use the **same background and crop** for everyone.
3. Record the **lighting setup** so future hires can be matched.
